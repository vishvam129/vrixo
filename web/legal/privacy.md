# Privacy Policy — Vrixo

*Last updated: 2026-04-11*

## What We Collect

| Category | Examples | Purpose | Retention |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Account data** | Email, password hash | Authentication | Until account deletion |
| **Uploaded images** | Photos you upload | AI processing only | **24 hours** — auto-deleted |
| **Processed images** | Your results | Display in your history | 7 days (free) / 30 days (Pro) |
| **Usage logs** | Feature used, timestamp | Quota tracking, abuse prevention | 90 days |
| **Payment info** | Handled by Razorpay / Stripe | Subscription billing | As required by law |
| **Diagnostic data** | Crash reports, errors | Bug fixing | 30 days |
| **Analytics** | Anonymized usage patterns | Product improvement | 12 months |

## What We DON'T Collect

- ❌ Your real name (unless you volunteer it)
- ❌ Your location (precise)
- ❌ Contact list or messages
- ❌ Biometric data beyond what's needed to process the photo you uploaded
- ❌ **We do NOT train our AI models on your photos**

## How We Use Your Data

1. **Provide the Service** — process your images through AI pipelines
2. **Enforce quotas** — track daily usage per user
3. **Improve the product** — aggregated, anonymized analytics only
4. **Prevent abuse** — detect CSAM, copyright violations, fraud
5. **Billing** — process subscription payments
6. **Communication** — send transactional emails (receipts, password resets)

## Who We Share With

- **Payment processors** (Razorpay, Stripe) — only billing info
- **Cloud infrastructure** (Cloudflare R2, Supabase) — encrypted storage
- **Email provider** (Resend) — transactional emails only
- **Content moderation** (PhotoDNA) — hash-only comparisons for CSAM detection
- **Law enforcement** — only when required by law and only for CSAM

**We never sell your data.**

## Your Rights

You have the right to:

- **Access** — download all your data
- **Delete** — permanently remove your account and data
- **Correct** — update your profile information
- **Export** — take your data with you
- **Object** — opt out of marketing emails at any time
- **Restrict** — limit how we process your data

Request any of these by emailing **privacy@vrixo.com**.

## Children

Vrixo is not intended for children under 13. Face-editing features require age 18+. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

## International Users

- **EU residents** — You are protected under GDPR.
- **Indian residents** — You are protected under the DPDP Act 2023.
- **California residents** — You are protected under CCPA.

## Security

- Passwords are hashed with PBKDF2 (100k iterations)
- All traffic uses TLS 1.3
- Uploaded images are encrypted at rest
- Access to production systems requires 2FA

## Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be announced via email and in-app notification.
